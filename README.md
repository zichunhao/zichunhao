# Hi, I'm Zichun 👋

I'm a Ph.D. candidate in physics at [Caltech](https://pma.caltech.edu/), working on machine learning and experimental high energy physics with the [CMS experiment](https://cms.cern/) at CERN.

## What I work on

- 🔭 [RINO](https://github.com/zichunhao/RINO), a step towards self-supervised foundation models for jets. The paper was a spotlight at the ML4PS workshop at NeurIPS 2025.
- 🧲 The [boosted Higgs boson pair search](https://github.com/LPC-HH/HH4b) in the four bottom quark final state at CMS, where I am one of the main contributors.
- 🎓 Before Caltech I did my undergrad at UC San Diego in the [Duarte Lab](https://jduarte.physics.ucsd.edu/), building [Lorentz group equivariant autoencoders](https://github.com/zichunhao/lgn-autoencoder) and contributing to [JetNet](https://github.com/jet-net/JetNet).
